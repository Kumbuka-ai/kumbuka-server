Before you continue

Please review and accept the kumbuka terms to use the memory console.

1 · Shared memory

The console stores structured team memories that the assistant reads and writes. Anything you add to a shared scope is visible to your team and to admins of this workspace.

2 · Private memory

Your private scope is owned by you. It is never exposed to admins, to this console, or through the connector — by design, not by configuration.

3 · Acceptable use

Don’t store credentials, secrets, or personal data of others in shared scopes. Memories are retained per the workspace’s retention policy.

4 · Identity

Sign-in is handled by your organization’s identity provider. You’re responsible for keeping your second factor secure.
